What Flower Resume does

Free AI resume tailoring. Paste a job description, see how well your resume covers its keywords, get a tailored draft from your saved profile, and download unlimited watermark-free PDFs. You need a free account. No credit card.

What it doesn't do (yet)

Cover letters, Word export, a job tracker or browser extension, or human resume review. If you need those, the comparisons above show which tools offer them.

Newer comparisons (Teal, Rezi, Kickresume) were checked against each vendor's own pricing pages on November 24, 2026. Prices change, so always confirm on the vendor's site.
