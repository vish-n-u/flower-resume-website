Head-to-head

AI tailoring — Flower Resume wins

On Kickresume's free plan, the AI Resume Writer isn't included. You get templates and pre-written phrases. With Premium, its AI can take the job ad as input, and reviewers describe a Resume Tailoring feature that rewrites your resume for a specific posting. Flower Resume does this for free, and shows a live score for how well your resume covers the posting's keywords.

Design and templates — Kickresume wins

Kickresume has 40 templates on Premium, 26 fonts and far more design control. It's one of the better-looking builders available. Flower Resume offers 5 simpler templates, including single-column Classic and Minimal layouts that are safest for ATS parsing and the quick skim recruiters do. If visual design matters for your field, Kickresume offers much more.

Everything beyond the resume — Kickresume wins

Kickresume also covers cover letters, a personal website, a mobile app and other career tools. Flower Resume does one thing: tailored resumes.

Price — Flower Resume wins, unless you're a student

Kickresume Premium costs $24 for a single month. It's much cheaper on a yearly plan, at $8/month billed $96. Students can get 6 months of Premium free. For everyone else who just needs tailored resumes, Flower Resume costs nothing.
