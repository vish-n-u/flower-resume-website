Head-to-head

Free plan — Flower Resume wins

Rezi's free plan gives you one resume and three PDF downloads. That's enough to try the product, but not to tailor a resume for each application. Flower Resume has no caps on resumes or downloads, and tailoring isn't limited by plan.

ATS analysis depth — Rezi wins

Rezi Score checks 23 metrics covering content, format and keyword targeting, and gives line-by-line feedback. Flower Resume's job-match score is narrower. It measures how well your resume covers the keywords in a specific posting, required and nice-to-have, and updates live as you edit. That's the part recruiters actually search on, but Rezi gives more detailed feedback overall.

Exports and extras — Rezi wins

Rezi exports to Word and Google Drive as well as PDF. It also writes unlimited cover letters, even on the free plan, and includes AI interview practice. Pro adds a monthly human review. Flower Resume exports PDF only and doesn't do cover letters.

Long-term cost — Depends on your search

If you job-hunt often, Rezi's $149 Lifetime plan is good value compared with $29/month. Note that Lifetime excludes the monthly expert review. If you don't want to pay at all, Flower Resume covers tailoring and unlimited PDFs for free.
