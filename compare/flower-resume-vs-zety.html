Pricing — Flower Resume wins

Zety's model is "free to build, pay to download." Plans run from ~$5.99–$23.70/month. Flower Resume charges nothing — ever. No trial, no expiry, no credit card.

Job Description Tailoring — Flower Resume wins

Flower Resume's core feature: paste a JD, get a tailored resume. Zety offers generic AI writing suggestions — not tied to any specific job. For competitive roles where keyword matching matters, the difference is significant.

Templates & Design — Zety wins

Zety has 18+ professionally designed templates. Flower Resume focuses on clean, ATS-safe formatting over visual variety. If design is your top priority and you're willing to pay, Zety has an edge here.

PDF Quality — Flower Resume wins (for the price)

Flower Resume PDFs are clean and watermark-free — for free. Zety's free PDFs are branded. At Zety's paid tier the quality is comparable, but Flower Resume's free output matches Zety's paid output.
