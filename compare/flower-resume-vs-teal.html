Head-to-head

Tailoring a resume to one posting — Flower Resume wins

This is what Flower Resume is built for. Paste a job description and it extracts every required and nice-to-have keyword, scores your resume against them live as you edit, and drafts a tailored version from your saved profile. There's no paid tier to unlock.

Teal also matches resumes to postings, but its free plan shows only the top 5 keywords. Unlimited keyword matching and unlimited AI writing are part of Teal+.

Running the whole job search — Teal wins

Teal's job tracker is its standout feature. You get unlimited saved jobs, contacts linked to each job, follow-up dates and stage checklists. Its Chrome extension, which reviewers consistently praise, captures postings directly from job boards. Flower Resume has nothing comparable. It's a resume tool, not a CRM for your search.

Cost if you need more than the free plan — Flower Resume wins

Teal+ is priced weekly, at $9/week, with cheaper per-day rates if you pay for 30 or 90 days. A long search can add up. Flower Resume doesn't have a paid tier. We apply an hourly fair-use limit on AI requests to prevent abuse, but no feature sits behind a paywall.

Cover letters — Teal wins

Teal can draft cover letters (one on the free plan, unlimited on Teal+). Flower Resume doesn't write cover letters today. Jobscan's 2025 data linked cover letters to a 3.4x higher interview rate, so if a role asks for one, that matters.

Choose Flower Resume if...

  • ✓ Your priority is matching each resume closely to the job description
  • ✓ You want unlimited AI tailoring without a weekly subscription
  • ✓ You apply to a moderate number of roles and track them elsewhere
  • ✓ You want no ads

Choose Teal if...

  • → You're managing dozens of applications and want a tracker and Chrome extension
  • → You need AI cover letters
  • → You like having jobs, contacts and resume versions in one workspace
  • → You're happy to pay $9/week while you search

Our verdict: Use Flower Resume to write each tailored resume, and use Teal's free tracker if you need pipeline management. They work well together. If you only want one tool and your search is large and organized, Teal's workspace is hard to beat. If you mainly need better-matched resumes, Flower Resume gives you more tailoring for free.
